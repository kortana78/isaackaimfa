Thinking About Thinking

29 June 2026

What is thinking?

How do thoughts come about?

How does a thought arise?

How did the thought "How do thoughts come about?" come about?

The other day, I was walking around when I had a beautiful idea. Then I asked myself: How did this beautiful idea come about?

Is every idea a product of past experience?

How does the brain organize everything it has experienced and produce new thoughts?

Before a thought appears in consciousness, is it already there in some form?

Do we really think?

Or am I only saying what is already there?

When I say, "I am thinking right now," am I actually thinking, or am I merely expressing something that already exists within me?
