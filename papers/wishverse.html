The Wishverse

A Formal Logical-Transition System over States    2026
Abstract We introduce the Wishverse, a formal mathematical framework for representing desire-driven state transformation. Stripping away physical interpretation, the Wishverse is defined as a triple (S, W, R) comprising a state space, a set of wish operators, and a rule system governing valid transitions. Wishes are modeled as non-deterministic functions mapping states to sets of successor states, forming a partial monoid under sequential composition. We establish four foundational axioms ensuring closure, logical consistency, transitional coherence, and well-foundedness under self-referential modification. We further develop notions of wish equivalence, impossibility, and meta-wishes, and identify the Wishverse as a full abstract rewriting system with branching operators. Three directions for further development are outlined: algebraic classification, modal logical embedding, and expressiveness analysis.
