  • Generalized Bawo: A Finite-State Model and an EXPTIME Upper Bound
    Preprint   2026
    Introduces Generalized-Bawo-Win, a finite-state two-player game model inspired by Malawian Bawo, parameterized by half-board width n and a binary-encoded lap cap L. Proves EXPTIME membership via a state-space bound, successor-generation bound, and WIN/LOSS/DRAW retrograde attractor. An upper bound only: no hardness result, and no classification of historical Bao.

  • The State-Space Complexity of Malawian Bawo: A Combinatorial Upper Bound
    Preprint   2026
    A finite-state model of the Malawian mancala variant Bawo, with an exact configuration-space count of approximately 1.7 × 1026 as an upper bound on the reachable state-space complexity. Separates configuration, legal, and reachable state spaces, and specifies a sampling methodology for the reachability estimate.

  • The Wishverse Under Adversarial Granting
    How Coarse Specification Breaks the Sufficiency of a Single Wish   2026
    Separates feasibility, preference, and expressibility in the naive wish model to recover betrayal and sequential value as two faces of one operator inequality. Under transitive feasibility, a single wish suffices if and only if the language separates successors; otherwise sequential wishing is unboundedly more valuable.
